# GBBookings: Software Requirements Specification, audit addendum

Date: 2026-11-10. Source: the four-phase code audit against Booking.com's public product.
This addendum carries forward IDs attributed to `docs/SRS-GBBookings-vs-Booking.com.pdf` and `todo.md`. The original PDF and separate audit report are not included in this checkout; their contents have not been independently verified here. The requirements below are self-contained, and the "Existing ID" column preserves the earlier cross-references.

## 1. Purpose and scope

GBBookings is a marketplace for hotels, homestays, car hire and tours in Gilgit-Baltistan. It serves Pakistan only.

In scope: the gaps and defects the audit found in the guest site, the partner dashboard, the admin back office and the platform underneath them.

Out of scope, by the owner's decision:

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Other currencies, exchange rates, foreign locales, several time zones, per-country tax | Pakistan only. PKR, Asia/Karachi and English/Urdu are correct as built. |
| SMS and WhatsApp notifications (MSG-09) | Not wanted |
| Several admin roles (ADM-17) | Not wanted |
| Passwordless sign-in, passkeys, data download, support bot, more languages, native apps | Not wanted |
| Booking without an account (AUTH-15), audit log viewer (SEC-09), CAPTCHA (SEC-05), SMS and authenticator codes (AUTH-06) | Deferred |

## 2. Constraints

- Free services only. No third-party error tracking; errors go to the server logs.
- No test scripts in the repository. Changes are verified with `tsc`, a build, and the running app.
- Existing navigation, footer and page sections are restyled, not removed, unless the owner confirms.
- Stack stays as it is: React and Vite, Express, MongoDB with transactions, Inngest, Safepay, Cloudinary, Resend, hosted on Vercel.
- Money is stored in paisa as integers. Dates are Pakistan calendar dates.

## 3. Users

| User | Goal |
|---|---|
| Guest | Find, book, pay for, change and review a stay, car or tour |
| Partner owner | List inventory, set prices and rules, see what is owed either way |
| Partner staff (manager, listings, reservations, finance) | Work the part of the business their role allows |
| Administrator | Approve partners and listings, handle disputes, refunds, statements and platform fees |

## 4. Current system in brief

One `Listing` model with a type (`hotel`, `homestay`, `car`, `tour`) and a specification block per type. One bookable unit model (`HotelRoomType`) and one per-date counter (`RoomNight`) serve all four: a room type, a car fleet, or a tour's seats. A booking holds exactly one unit type with a quantity. Bookings are created in a MongoDB transaction with guarded per-night counters and idempotency keys. Tenancy is one business per user, enforced in each service by scoping queries to the caller's vendor.

## 5. Functional requirements

Priority: P1 before public launch, P2 as volume grows, P3 later. Rate plans (FR-HTL-01) and multi-room checkout (FR-HTL-02) remain proposed launch scope pending the owner's decision, as recorded in `todo.md`; their P1 labels do not imply approval.

## 6. Non-functional requirements

These rows describe audit findings and proposed safeguards. Tasks and implementation status are in `todo.md` section 11. The separate audit report is unavailable in this checkout; use the acceptance criteria here and verify each claim against the current source before changing code.

| ID | Requirement | Measure | Audit item | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Guests behind a shared mobile-network address shall not lose their session because of other people's traffic. | Valid signed-in API traffic is counted per user. Refresh uses a separate per-session counter that survives token rotation and does not consume the sign-in or general API quota. Anonymous or invalid credentials retain IP limits. A throttled refresh, network failure or server outage never ends a known session; an invalid-session response does. | AUD-01 | P1 |
| NFR-02 | No guest request shall wait on housekeeping work. | Expiring stale bookings runs only in the scheduled job when Inngest is configured. | AUD-02 | P1 |
| NFR-03 | Calendar imports shall be attempted at least every 30 minutes under normal operation. | Demonstrate the attempt interval for 500 rooms with up to five feeds each using bounded concurrency and request timeouts. Measure attempt time separately from each feed's last successful import. Failed feeds retain their last known blocked dates, show a stale/error warning to the partner, and are retried on the next cycle. A stale feed does not automatically close otherwise available dates; disclose that external availability cannot be confirmed until a successful import. The target is an attempt interval, not a guarantee that an unavailable external feed stays fresh. | AUD-03 | P1 |
| NFR-04 | Search shall stay fast as the catalogue grows. | A dated search reads room types only for listings that already match the other filters. No search query scans listing descriptions with a regular expression. Target: under 500 ms at 5,000 listings. | AUD-04 | P2 |
| NFR-05 | One booking shall not be able to open unlimited payment sessions. | At most five payment attempts per booking. | AUD-05 | P1 |
| NFR-06 | One failing scheduled job shall not block or repeat successful jobs. | Each frequent job runs in its own named, parallel Inngest step. Connection failures and job-level exceptions reach Inngest for independent retries; completed steps are reused. Without Inngest, the timer runs every job and logs failures independently. | AUD-06 | P2 |
| NFR-07 | Finance screens shall avoid loading whole collections while preserving all money owed. | Use indexed queries and database aggregation. Date-filter period movements by their event dates: payments by paidAt, sent refunds by sentAt and duplicate payments by the duplicate refund record's createdAt. Outstanding refunds and unsettled partner balances include older items from every period. Verification includes an old unsettled booking, an old pending refund and an old booking refunded within the selected period. | AUD-07 | P2 |
| NFR-08 | Inventory counters shall be repairable. | A job recomputes `booked` from live bookings and reports any difference; a cancellation is never blocked for good by a wrong counter. | AUD-08 | P2 |
| NFR-09 | Tenant scoping shall live in one place. | Every partner service resolves its vendor through one shared function; one business per user is also guaranteed by a database index until FR-PRT-05 replaces it. | AUD-09 | P2 |
| NFR-10 | The type checker shall see the data model. | Models are typed from their schemas; `Model<any>` is gone from the booking, listing, room and vendor models first. | AUD-10 | P2 |
| NFR-11 | Booking references shall not collide in practice. | References carry at least 40 random bits, or creation retries on a duplicate. | AUD-11 | P3 |
| NFR-12 | Payment webhooks shall be authenticated. | The webhook checks the gateway's signature before doing any work. | AUD-12 | P3 |
| NFR-13 | Bulk operations shall be atomic per booking and report partial results. | FR-TUR-03 and FR-GST-02 commit each booking's status, inventory release, refund obligation and notification queue together. External refunds and emails happen after commit; their failures remain visible and retryable. Repeating a bulk action cannot duplicate refunds or notifications. There is no rollback promise across external services or across the entire batch. | new | P1 |
| NFR-14 | Identity data shall be private. | CNICs, licence photos and traveller lists use authenticated storage, are visible only to the roles that need them, and follow a stated retention period. | new | P1 |

## 7. Suggested order

1. NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-06, NFR-03, NFR-05: session stability, reliable scheduling, calendar capacity and bounded payment attempts.
2. FR-GST-01, FR-PRT-01, FR-PRT-02: getting paid and filing.
3. FR-HTL-03: correct extra-bed and extra-guest charging. Consider FR-HTL-01 and FR-HTL-02 together after their scope decision; correct charging does not depend on approving multi-room checkout.
4. FR-CAR-01, FR-TUR-01, FR-TUR-02, FR-TUR-03: so cars and tours match how local operators sell.
5. FR-GST-02 and the remaining P1 rows.
6. P2 rows as volume grows.
